## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How do I calculate if a chargeback is worth fighting in representment?
A: Multiply the total recoverable amount by your reason-code win rate, then subtract the internal labor cost of building the evidence packet and non-refundable gateway fees.

### Q: What is the Visa VAMP 0.90% threshold and how does it affect Shopify sellers?
A: Visa's Acquirer Monitoring Program combines fraud reports (TC40) and non-fraud disputes (TC15) divided by total settled transactions. Exceeding 0.90% triggers merchant monitoring.

### Q: Why does a 25% chargeback recovery fee hurt low-margin e-commerce brands?
A: A 25% fee is charged on gross order value, not net profit. If your store runs at a 22% net margin, winning a dispute through a 25% commission app still leaves you net negative.

### Q: When should I use Verifi RDR or Ethoca alerts instead of fighting a dispute?
A: Use RDR or Ethoca auto-refunds when the order value is below your representment break-even AOV or when your monthly VAMP dispute count is approaching the 0.90% ratio ceiling.

### Q: What does Visa Compelling Evidence 3.0 (CE 3.0) require for Reason Code 10.4?
A: Visa CE 3.0 requires at least two prior undisputed transactions from the same cardholder more than 120 days old sharing two matching data elements like IP address or device ID.
