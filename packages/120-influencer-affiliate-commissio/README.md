## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: Why do brands lose money when stacking influencer discount codes with affiliate commissions?
A: When you give a creator a 20% discount code for their audience AND a 20% affiliate commission, your effective revenue reduction is 36% (on a Net basis) or 40% (if commission is mistakenly calculated on the pre-discount Gross price). On a brand with a 55% gross margin before shipping and payment fees, that leaves less than 10% net operating profit.

### Q: Should affiliate commissions be calculated on Gross or Net order value?
A: Always on Net Merchandise Value—meaning after promo code discounts are deducted, and excluding shipping charges and sales tax/VAT. Calculating a 20% commission on the pre-discount retail price overpays the affiliate by 3% to 5% of collected revenue on every single order.

### Q: How do I account for gifted PR seeding boxes when calculating influencer ROI?
A: You must include the landed COGS + outbound fulfillment cost of all seeded kits—including the 60%–80% of gifted creators who never post—and divide that total seeding pool by the net contribution profit per affiliate order to find your true break-even order count.

### Q: How do I stop paying affiliate commissions on returned or canceled orders?
A: Never pay affiliate commissions immediately upon checkout. Set a 30-to-45-day commission locking window that exceeds your customer return policy, and reconcile refunded order IDs before exporting your monthly payout batch.
