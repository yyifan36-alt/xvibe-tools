## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: Why do so many international customers refuse DDU packages at the door?
A: Because carriers like DHL, FedEx, and UPS charge an extra $15 to €18 'disbursement/advancement fee' just for fronting the VAT at customs. On a $90 order, a buyer hit with $18 VAT plus a $16 carrier fee ($34 total at the door) often refuses delivery.

### Q: What happens when an EU order crosses the €150 IOSS threshold?
A: Up to €150 intrinsic value, EU orders qualify for Import One-Stop Shop (IOSS): you collect VAT at checkout, pay zero customs duty, and clear customs instantly. Over €150, IOSS no longer applies and formal duty + brokerage fees kick in.

### Q: How much do FX conversion and international card fees add to cross-border orders?
A: Most payment gateways add 1.0%–1.5% for international credit cards plus 1.5%–2.0% for currency conversion (FX spread), bringing total payment processing from 2.9% to roughly 5.9%–6.4%.

### Q: Should I bake DDP duties into the product price or show them as a separate checkout line?
A: In VAT-inclusive markets like the UK, EU, and Australia, baking VAT into a clean local price (e.g., £89 Tax Included) converts significantly better than adding a surprise 22% tax line on the final payment step.
