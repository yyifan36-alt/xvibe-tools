## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How do I calculate my break-even CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) for dropshipping?
A: Subtract your supplier product cost, fulfillment/shipping fee, payment gateway fee (typically 2.9% + $0.30), and expected refund/chargeback allowance from your Average Order Value (AOV). The remaining dollar amount is your Break-Even Max CPA.

### Q: Why am I losing money with a 2.5x ROAS on Meta or TikTok ads?
A: Because ROAS only compares gross revenue to ad spend. If your landed product + shipping cost, payment fees, and refunds consume 62% of your selling price, your break-even ROAS is 2.63x—meaning a 2.50x ROAS loses money on every order.

### Q: How should I account for dropshipping refunds and chargebacks in my daily P&L?
A: When a customer refunds an order, you lose the entire ad CPA, the non-refundable payment processing fee, and often the outbound shipping cost. Setting aside a 3% to 6% revenue reserve gives you an accurate picture of take-home cash.

### Q: Does DropProfit Studio require connecting my Shopify or TikTok Shop API?
A: No. Unlike cloud apps that sync your winning SKUs and supplier links to external servers, DropProfit Studio executes 100% locally inside your browser.

### Q: How can I increase my allowable CPA ceiling without changing suppliers?
A: Offering 2-pack or 3-pack quantity bundles increases Average Order Value (AOV) faster than incremental shipping weight, often expanding your break-even CPA ceiling by $12 to $25 per order.
