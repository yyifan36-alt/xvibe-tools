## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: Can I opt out of Etsy Offsite Ads once my shop passes $10,000 in annual sales?
A: No. Once your Etsy shop makes $10,000 USD or more in any consecutive 365-day period, Etsy permanently locks your account into Mandatory Offsite Ads at the discounted 12% fee rate for the lifetime of your shop—even if your revenue drops below $10,000 in future years.

### Q: Does Etsy charge the 12% or 15% Offsite Ads fee on shipping and gift wrapping?
A: Yes. Just like Etsy's 6.5% Transaction Fee, the 12% or 15% Offsite Ads fee is calculated on the total amount the buyer pays—including item price, buyer-paid shipping, and gift wrapping (capped at $100 maximum Offsite Ad fee per order).

### Q: How does Etsy's 30-day Offsite Ads attribution window affect repeat customers?
A: If a shopper clicks an Etsy Offsite Ad for one of your listings on Google, Pinterest, or Instagram and purchases anything from your shop within 30 days—including multiple separate orders or a completely different item—every order placed inside that 30-day window is charged the 12% or 15% Offsite Ads fee.

### Q: Why shouldn't I raise my Etsy prices by +12% across the board to cover Offsite Ads?
A: Because Offsite Ads typically account for only 15% to 25% of a shop's total orders. Raising your prices by a full +12% punishes the 80% of buyers who find you organically in Etsy search and tanks your conversion rate. Instead, a +2.6% to +3.4% blended price gross-up across all orders recovers 100% of the Offsite Ads fee.

### Q: Why are Print-on-Demand (Printify/Printful) Etsy sellers hit hardest by Mandatory Offsite Ads?
A: Print-on-Demand apparel often runs on a 30% to 35% gross margin before Etsy fees. When an order triggers a 22.4% combined Etsy + Offsite Ads fee stack on top of a 65% Printify production + shipping invoice, the seller is left with less than $1.50 net profit—or a net loss if running a 15% sale.

### Q: Does EtsyOffsite Studio connect to our Etsy shop or share our bestseller SKUs?
A: Never. Unlike cloud Etsy research extensions that harvest your shop data, all fee calculations and CSV audits run 100% locally in your browser RAM.
