### Q: How many linear feet per pound do you get from 5-inch vs. 6-inch .032 seamless aluminum gutter coil?
A: Standard 5-inch K-style gutter uses 11.75-inch wide coil: .027 gauge yields ~3.20 ft/lb and .032 gauge yields ~2.70 ft/lb. Standard 6-inch K-style gutter uses 15.0-inch wide coil: .027 gauge yields ~2.42 ft/lb and heavy .032 gauge yields ~2.05 ft/lb (requiring 31.7% more aluminum weight per linear foot than 5-inch).

### Q: Why does quoting a flat per-foot price without counting miters and downspouts lose money?
A: Running a straight 60-foot rear eave off an IronMan roll-former takes 20 minutes, whereas a 60-foot front elevation with 6 bay-window turret miters, 3 downspouts, and 2nd-story ladder moves takes 2.5+ hours. Charging $30–$45 per miter and pricing 3x4 downspouts separately protects crew hourly yield.

### Q: What is a healthy profit margin on stainless steel micro-mesh gutter guard installations?
A: Wholesale contractor-grade aluminum-framed stainless micro-mesh guards cost $3.60 to $4.85 per linear foot (plus zip screws and end caps). Installed at $20–$28/ln.ft. alongside new seamless gutters (or $24–$34/ln.ft. as a standalone retrofit with gutter cleaning), gross material margin exceeds 75%.
