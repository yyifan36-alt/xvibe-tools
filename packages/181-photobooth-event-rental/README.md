## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: What are Idle Hours in a photo booth rental contract and how much should I charge?
A: Idle hours (or downtime) occur when the client or venue requires the photo booth to be completely set up hours before the active rental begins—or paused during wedding dinner and speeches. Industry standard idle time is billed at $50 to $75 per hour to cover your attendant's waiting wage and locked equipment.

### Q: How much does DNP dye-sublimation photo booth paper and ribbon cost per print?
A: For industry-workhorse printers like the DNP DS620A or QW410, a 2-roll media case (800 total 4x6 prints) costs ~$135 to $148. Each 4x6 print costs ~$0.17 to $0.19 (yielding two 2x6 strips at ~$0.09 each), while metallic or perforated media runs ~$0.26 per 4x6.

### Q: How many events does it take to pay off a 360 photo booth or DSLR print booth?
A: A commercial 360 video booth platform with flight case, iPad/GoPro, RGB tube lights, and stanchions ($2,200–$3,200) typically pays for itself in 3 to 4 bookings at $850/event. A flagship DSLR + DNP DS620A print shell + sequin/pillow backdrop ($4,800–$6,200) pays back in 6 to 8 weddings.

### Q: Why are unattended digital drop-off iPad booths so profitable for multi-unit operators?
A: Because a digital SMS/AirDrop/QR iPad booth has no printer paper jams and no spinning 360 arm safety liability, it doesn't require an on-site attendant. One operator can drop off 4 units between 11 AM and 3 PM for $500–$650 each and pick them up at midnight or next morning.

### Q: How should I pay photo booth attendants for weekend events?
A: Pay attendants $22 to $30 per hour portal-to-portal (from warehouse load-in through setup, active event hours, teardown, and warehouse return), plus $0.67/mile if they transport the booth in their own SUV.
