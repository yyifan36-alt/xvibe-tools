### Q: How much do Nayax and Cantaloupe charge for vending machine credit card processing?

### Q: What is a healthy profit margin for a vending machine or smart cooler route?
A: Following the industry '2-to-1 Rule' (pricing items at minimum 2.2x landed wholesale cost, or 42%–46% COGS), well-run routes clear 45% to 52% net operating profit before vehicle depreciation when Dual-Pricing is active and location commissions are kept under 5%.

### Q: When should a vending operator pay a commission to a location owner?
A: Never offer a revenue-share commission to locations generating under $1,000/month per machine—your service is an employee retention amenity. For high-volume factories, hospitals, or hotels doing $2,000+/month, offer 5% to 10% of NET sales (after product tax, deposits, and card fees) or a sliding threshold.

### Q: What is pre-kitting in a vending machine business?
A: Pre-kitting uses live MDB/DEX telemetry from your card reader to pack the exact SKUs and quantities needed for a specific machine into a tote at your warehouse before driving—cutting on-site stop time from two 20-minute trips (check then fill) down to a single 11-minute stop.
