A: To hit a 4% active sodium hypochlorite mix on heavy Gloeocapsa magma roof algae, roughly 32% of your total spray volume is 12.5% SH. A 10,000 sq.ft commercial roof typically requires 30 to 40 gallons of bulk 12.5% SH.

### Q: What is the standard commercial pressure washing rate per square foot?
A: Unheated concrete flatwork typically bids at $0.12 to $0.18/sq.ft for large retail lots (15,000+ sq.ft), while greased restaurant drive-thrus, gas station aprons, and hot-water gum removal command $0.24 to $0.35/sq.ft.

### Q: How much fuel does a hot-water pressure washer rig burn per hour?
A: An 8 GPM skid running a twin-cylinder gas engine (GX690/IGX800) plus a 12V Beckett diesel burner consumes roughly 1.1 gallons of gasoline and 1.7 gallons of diesel per continuous trigger hour (~$10.50–$12.50/hr).
