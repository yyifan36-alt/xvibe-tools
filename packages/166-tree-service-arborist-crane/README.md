## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How much should a 3-to-4 person tree service crew gross per day?
A: To cover a bucket truck, 18-inch drum chipper, mini skid-steer, diesel, Class 0106 Workers' Comp, and general liability insurance while netting 25%–35% profit, a professional 3-to-4 person crew must bill $3,200 to $4,800+ per 8-hour day ($400–$600/hr gross).

### Q: When does it make financial sense to rent a crane for a tree removal?
A: Any time a crane cuts on-site crew time by 5+ hours (half a crew day). Even if a 38-ton or 50-ton crane with operator costs $240/hr ($960 for a 4-hour minimum), saving 6 hours of a 4-man crew plus equipment ($1,500+ in internal cost) nets you more profit and frees the afternoon for another job.

### Q: How much does Class 0106 Workers' Compensation add to tree crew labor?
A: Depending on your state and Experience Modification Rate (EMR), NCCI Class 0106 (Tree Pruning, Spraying, Repairing) adds 16% to 28% on top of gross wages, plus another 8.5% for FICA/FUTA/SUTA payroll taxes.

### Q: How do arborists price stump grinding accurately?
A: Standard stump grinding is priced at $4.00 to $7.50 per inch of widest root-flare diameter (not trunk DBH), with a mandatory $175 to $250 minimum machine mobilization fee and an extra 40% surcharge if chip haul-away and topsoil backfill are requested.

### Q: How much do wood chipper knives, anvil wear, and diesel cost per day?
A: Running a 130+ HP diesel 15" or 18" drum/disc chipper burns 10 to 16 gallons of off-road diesel per active day ($40–$65) plus roughly $35 to $50/day in amortized knife sharpening, replacement bolts, hydraulic filters, and anvil maintenance.
