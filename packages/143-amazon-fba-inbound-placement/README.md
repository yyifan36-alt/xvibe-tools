A: You must select the Amazon-Optimized Shipment Splits option (typically 4+ destinations) and pack at least 5 identical master cartons per item with matching SKU quantities.

### Q: When is paying the 1-warehouse FBA placement fee actually cheaper?
A: For small restocks under 400 units or LTL shipments from an overseas port to a nearby West Coast FC, paying $0.27 to $0.45 per unit often beats paying 4 separate LTL minimums.

### Q: Does Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) waive FBA placement fees?
A: Yes. Inventory routed through AWD and auto-replenished into FBA fulfillment centers receives a 100% waiver on FBA inbound placement service fees.

A: If your shipping plan has fewer than 5 identical cartons per SKU or mixes different SKU quantities across boxes, Amazon hides the zero-fee optimized split option.

A: For Minimal Shipment Splits (1 location), fees typically range from $0.21 to $0.68 per unit for standard-size items and $1.58 to $5.95+ per unit for large bulky items.
