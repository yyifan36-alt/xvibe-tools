A: Amazon requires sellers to maintain a Pre-Fulfillment Cancel Rate below 2.5%. Canceling seller-fulfilled orders due to out-of-stock errors strips your Buy Box share, penalizes organic ranking, and risks FBM listing deactivation.

### Q: Why shouldn't I set the same 20% holdback buffer on every sales channel?
