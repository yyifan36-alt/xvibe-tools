## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: Why does a lower factory unit price on a 5,000-unit packaging MOQ often reduce net profit?
A: When you step up from 1,500 to 5,000 custom boxes to save $0.40 per unit, you tie up thousands in extra upfront cash and store 4 to 8 additional pallets of flat-packed cartons at your 3PL for 6–12 months. At $30–$45 per pallet per month, cumulative 3PL storage fees frequently erase 50% to 75% of the factory bulk discount.

### Q: How should ecommerce sellers amortize one-time die-cut tooling and printing plates?
A: Always amortize 100% of one-time structural die-cut molds, CMYK flexo/litho plates, or rotogravure cylinders across your FIRST production PO rather than assuming future reorders. If you change box dimensions or branding on reorder #2, unamortized tooling becomes a sunk loss.

### Q: How can switching to custom rigid boxes trigger a carrier dimensional weight (DIM) surcharge?
A: Custom rigid boxes and protective inserts add 0.5 to 1.5 inches to outer dimensions compared to poly mailers or snug corrugated mailers. Crossing USPS Cubic tiers or the 139 cu in/lb UPS/FedEx dimensional divisor can add $1.95 to $3.80 in outbound shipping cost on every order—dwarfing the cost of the box itself.

### Q: Does PackMOQ Studio upload our overseas factory quotes or supplier contacts to any server?
A: Never. Every MOQ tier calculation, tooling amortization table, and CSV export executes 100% locally inside your browser. Your supplier pricing and packaging BOM remain completely private on your device.

### Q: Can I compare custom stand-up pouches with rotogravure cylinder fees against digital print runs?
A: Yes. Digital pouch printing has $0 plate fees and low 500-unit MOQs but higher unit costs, whereas rotogravure printing requires $500–$900 in copper cylinder fees across 5,000+ units. Our studio calculates the exact crossover volume where cylinder amortization pays off.
