At 1,100 billable hours/year, a $40/hr tech plus $140,000 in van/marketing/office overhead actually costs your business ~$225 per billable hour just to break even.

### Q: What is a healthy billable wrench-time efficiency percentage for residential service technicians?
A: Most residential HVAC, plumbing, and electrical service trucks run between 48% and 58% billable efficiency ( roughly 4.0 to 4.6 billable flat-rate task hours per 8-hour shift, across 3 to 4 service calls per day).

### Q: How should I mark up wholesale HVAC and plumbing parts in a flat-rate pricebook?
A: Use a sliding-scale divisor markup based on part cost: divide small parts under $25 by 0.20 (5.0x multiplier), mid-range parts ($25–$250) by 0.35 (2.85x), and major assemblies ($250–$1,000+) by 0.50 to 0.55—always including an 8% warranty callback reserve.

### Q: How do I price my 15%-Off Service Agreement Club without losing margin?
A: Calculate your flat-rate task price at your full target net margin (e.g., 50% margin = $380) and make THAT your Club Member price. Then divide $380 by 0.85 ($447) to establish your Non-Member Standard Retail price.

### Q: How much does a warranty callback actually cost an HVAC or plumbing company?
A: A single 90-minute no-charge callback costs you both the $340 in direct burdened tech + van cost AND the $550+ in lost opportunity revenue from the paying call that slot could have served—nearly $890 total.
