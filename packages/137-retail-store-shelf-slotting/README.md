## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How do retailer margin and distributor margin stack in the CPG pricing waterfall?
A: They compound sequentially down from MSRP. On a $10.00 MSRP with a 45% retailer margin, the retailer pays the distributor $5.50. If the distributor takes a 22% margin, they pay your brand $4.29—before broker fees or trade spend.

### Q: Why does a '1 Free Case Per Store' (Free-Fill) cost way more than my manufacturing COGS?
A: When you ship through a distributor, the distributor still buys the case from you and bills you back via a Manufacturer Chargeback (MCB) at the wholesale price PLUS a 10%–15% handling fee—turning a $20 COGS case into a $58 invoice deduction.

### Q: What is a healthy Trade Spend percentage for an emerging retail brand?
A: Most retail brands must budget 12% to 20% of gross wholesale revenue for trade spend (including temporary price reductions, scan-backs, distributor shows, and spoilage allowances).

### Q: What does UPSW mean and why do retail buyers care about it?
A: UPSW stands for Units Per Store Per Week (also called velocity). Retailers track dollar velocity per linear foot; if your UPSW falls below category benchmarks during the 6-month category review, you get delisted before recovering your slotting fee.

### Q: Do I need to connect an ERP or upload confidential distributor contracts?
A: Never. All margin waterfall and slotting payback calculations run 100% locally in your browser.
