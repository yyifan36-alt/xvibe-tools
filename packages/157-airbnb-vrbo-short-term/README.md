## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: Why do I lose money on my cleaning fee even when I charge guests the exact amount my cleaner bills me?
A: Because both Airbnb and Vrbo charge their host commission on the total reservation amount—which includes your nightly rate AND the guest cleaning fee. If your cleaner charges $160 and you set a $160 cleaning fee on Vrbo (8% fee) or Airbnb Host-Only (15.5% fee), the platform deducts $12.80 to $24.80 from that cleaning fee, forcing you to subsidize every turnover out of pocket.

### Q: What happens to my Airbnb fee when I connect a PMS like Guesty, Hospitable, or Hostaway?
A: Airbnb automatically switches most API-connected PMS listings from the traditional Split-Fee model (3% host fee + ~14.2% guest service fee) to the mandatory Host-Only Fee model (15% to 16% deducted entirely from the host payout, with 0% guest service fee). If you forget to gross up your Airbnb channel markup by ~14.6% inside your PMS, your net payout drops overnight.

### Q: How does Vrbo's 8% Pay-Per-Booking fee actually work?
A: Vrbo's Pay-Per-Booking structure stacks a 5% commission on the rental amount and cleaning fee PLUS a 3% payment processing fee charged on the entire guest payment—including state and local occupancy taxes in many jurisdictions. That makes Vrbo's effective take rate 8.3% to 8.6% of your gross revenue.

### Q: Why do 1-night and 2-night stays often have lower net profit despite higher nightly rates?
A: Short stays multiply turnover friction: if you subsidize $35 of the cleaner's invoice to stay competitive in total-price search, spend $18 on coffee/toiletries/linen laundry, and absorb higher HVAC/utility spikes during check-in/check-out, a 1-night stay loses $53+ in fixed turnover drag that a 4-night stay amortizes over four days.

### Q: How should property owners structure co-host or property manager commission agreements?
A: Always define co-host commission (typically 15%–20%) on Net Room Revenue (Nightly Rate minus Airbnb/Vrbo Host Fees, excluding the Cleaning Fee pass-through). Allowing a manager to take 20% of the cleaning fee means you pay $192 ($160 cleaner + $32 manager cut) on a $160 guest cleaning charge.

### Q: Does STRYield Studio connect to our Airbnb account or upload owner tax ledgers?
A: Never. All channel fee comparisons, turnover deficit checks, and CSV exports run 100% locally in your browser RAM.
