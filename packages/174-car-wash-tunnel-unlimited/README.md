A: Despite having 'unlimited' access, the average retail club member washes 2.8 to 3.3 times per month year-round (peaking around 3.8x during spring pollen or winter road-salt months and dipping to 2.4x during rainy months).

A: For a well-calibrated 110-to-130 foot express tunnel with water reclaim, direct variable cost per car typically runs $1.10 to $1.65: roughly $0.55 to $0.95 in chemicals (presoak, soap, ceramic/graphene, drying agent), $0.25 to $0.40 in fresh water/sewer, and $0.25 to $0.30 in conveyor/blower electricity.
