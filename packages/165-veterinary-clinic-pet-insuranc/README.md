## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: What is the difference between pet insurance and a clinic wellness plan?
A: Pet insurance (such as Trupanion, Nationwide, or Pumpkin) is underwritten risk coverage for unexpected accidents and illnesses, whereas an in-house clinic wellness plan is a pre-paid monthly subscription for predictable routine care (exams, vaccines, wellness bloodwork, and preventive dentals).

### Q: How do veterinary clinics prevent pet owners from using all wellness services in Month 1 and canceling?
A: Best-practice clinics charge a non-refundable $65 to $95 enrollment fee, require a 12-month auto-draft agreement, and state in the contract that early cancellation bills the client the remaining retail value of services already rendered.

### Q: How should DVM ProSal production be calculated on wellness plan payments?
A: Never pay DVM production on the monthly auto-draft fee itself. Instead, credit the attending veterinarian 20%–22% of the discounted internal fee-for-service code at the exact visit when the exam, blood panel, or dental is performed.

### Q: How much do third-party veterinary wellness plan software platforms charge?
A: Dedicated wellness billing platforms typically charge a base clinic fee plus $3.50 to $5.50 per enrolled pet per month, on top of 2.9% + $0.30 credit card processing fees and failed-payment retry charges.

### Q: Does Trupanion VetDirect Pay charge the veterinary hospital an extra merchant fee?
A: Trupanion's direct-pay integration settles the insurer's portion (up to 90% of covered invoices after deductible) directly to the hospital within minutes at standard or lower processing cost, leaving the pet owner to pay only their 10% coinsurance at checkout.

### Q: Does this calculator require connecting to ezyVet, Cornerstone, or Shepherd?
A: No. All veterinary wellness plan pricing, lab COGS, and DVM ProSal calculations run 100% locally in your browser with zero practice management login required.
