## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How do Printify and Printful multi-area print surcharges affect my Etsy or Shopify profit margin?
A: Adding a back print ($4.50–$5.95), sleeve print ($2.20–$3.50), or inside neck label ($1.20–$2.00) directly increases your fulfillment cost AND increases the retail price base on which Etsy or Shopify calculates percentage transaction fees. PodMargin Studio models every print location separately.

### Q: When is upgrading to Printify Premium ($29/month) worth it for a POD store?
A: Printify Premium reduces blank garment costs by up to 20% (typically saving $1.80 to $3.40 per shirt or hoodie). For most sellers, shipping just 10 to 15 garments per month covers the $29 subscription cost. Our studio calculates your exact break-even order count from your SKU catalog.

### Q: Does Etsy charge its 6.5% transaction fee on the shipping price I charge customers?
A: Yes. Etsy applies its 6.5% transaction fee, 3%+$0.25 payment processing fee, and 12%–15% Offsite Ads fee to the combined total of item price plus customer-paid shipping. Our calculator automatically computes fees on the combined gross order total.

### Q: Why do my 2XL, 3XL, and 4XL shirt variants have lower profit margins?
A: POD print providers charge $2.00 to $5.50 more for 2XL–5XL blanks and heavier shipping weight tiers. If you set one flat retail price across S–4XL, extended sizes often lose money when combined with ad spend.

A: Never. PodMargin Studio runs 100% locally in your browser tab. Your best-selling niche designs, SKU sales volumes, and supplier margins never leave your computer.
