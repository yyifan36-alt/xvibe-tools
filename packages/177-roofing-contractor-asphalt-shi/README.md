## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How much shingle waste factor should I add to an EagleView or Hover measurement?
A: For a simple open gable roof, add 10% to 12% waste (including starter and ridge if not ordered separately). For standard suburban hip roofs, add 14% to 16%, and for complex cut-up hip roofs with multiple valleys, turrets, and dormers, add 18% to 21%.

### Q: How does the 10/50/50 commission split work for roofing sales reps?
A: Under a 10/50/50 split on a $18,000 roof: the company first deducts 10% ($1,800) for office/insurance overhead plus actual direct job costs (e.g., $10,200 for materials, crew labor, EagleView, permit, and dumpster). The remaining $6,000 gross profit is split 50/50 ($3,000 to the rep, $3,000 + $1,800 = $4,800 retained by the company).

### Q: Wait—do roofing crews get paid on Net Measured Squares or Ordered Squares?
A: Most subcontractor roofing crews demand to be paid on the Ordered Bundle Squares (e.g., 37 squares delivered by ABC Supply on a 32-square measured roof) because cutting hips and valleys requires extra labor. Always clarify whether your crew's $/square rate is based on EagleView net squares or delivered bundle squares.

### Q: How much does a 30-square asphalt shingle tear-off weigh in a dumpster?
A: Standard architectural shingles plus felt, nails, and ridge cap weigh 230 to 250 lbs per square. A single-layer 32-square tear-off weighs roughly 3.7 to 4.0 tons (7,400–8,000 lbs)—meaning a roll-off dumpster with a 2-ton cap will incur ~2 tons ($180+) in overweight tipping fees.

### Q: How many free sheets of OSB decking should a roofing contract include?
A: Best-practice retail contracts include 2 to 3 sheets of 7/16" OSB decking replacement in the base bid, with a pre-agreed contract clause billing $85 to $110 per additional rotted sheet (documented via timestamped CompanyCam photos during tear-off).
