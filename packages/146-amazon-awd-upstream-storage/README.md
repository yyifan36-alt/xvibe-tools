### Q: How does Amazon AWD waive FBA Inbound Placement Service Fees and Low-Inventory-Level Fees?
A: When you send inventory to an Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) facility first, Amazon handles the downstream transfer into FBA fulfillment centers. Every unit replenished from AWD into FBA receives a 100% waiver on FBA Inbound Placement Service Fees—and if 70%+ of your SKU volume auto-replenishes through AWD, Low-Inventory-Level and Overstock fees are waived too.

### Q: When is shipping directly to FBA cheaper than using Amazon AWD?
A: AWD charges $2.70 per box round-trip ($1.35 inbound + $1.35 outbound) plus $1.00–$1.15/cu.ft transportation to FBA. If you ship fast-selling SKUs (<30 days of supply) in small cartons that already qualify for the 5-identical-carton zero-placement-fee FBA split, direct FBA beats AWD.

### Q: What is the AWD 70% Auto-Replenishment Integrated Rate discount?
A: When at least 70% of a SKU's FBA inventory over the preceding 90 days is sent via AWD auto-replenishment, Amazon discounts AWD storage by 25% (from $0.48 to $0.36/cu.ft/month) and reduces AWD-to-FBA transportation by 13% (to $1.00/cu.ft).

### Q: How much does AWD save during Q4 Peak Season (October–December)?
A: Direct FBA storage triples from $0.78/cu.ft to $2.40/cu.ft/month during Q4. AWD upstream storage remains $0.48/cu.ft ($0.36/cu.ft integrated) year-round—saving up to 85% on seasonal buffer stock.
