## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: Why does dividing total fence footage by 8 always short your post and concrete order?
A: Every straight stretch between corners, ends, or gates requires (Length ÷ 8) + 1 posts, and any short panel cut under 8 feet still requires a full terminal post. A 180-foot yard split across 5 stretches with 2 gates takes 28–30 posts, not 23.

### Q: How many 80-lb bags of concrete are required per 4x4 or 5x5 fence post hole?
A: A standard 10-inch diameter auger hole dug 36 inches deep for a 5x5 vinyl or 4x4 wood post requires 2.0 to 2.4 bags of 80-lb concrete mix (or 3.2+ bags for a 12-inch x 42-inch heavy gate/corner post hole).

### Q: How should fence contractors price single walk gates and double-drive gates?
A: Never roll gates into your linear-foot price. Charge $375–$495 per 4-ft walk gate and $825–$1,150 per 8-ft to 10-ft double-drive gate to cover internal aluminum post stiffeners, welded gate frames, heavy-duty hinges, and extra leveling labor.
