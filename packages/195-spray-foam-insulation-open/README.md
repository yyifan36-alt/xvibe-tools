## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How do you calculate board feet and 55-gallon A/B drum sets for spray foam insulation?
A: Multiply surface area (sq.ft.) by foam thickness in inches to get Net Board Feet (1 sq.ft. at 1 inch thick = 1 board foot). Add 12%–16% for stud-flush scarfing and over-spray, then divide by your real field drum yield (~16,500 bd.ft./set for 0.5-lb open-cell; ~4,250 bd.ft./set for 2.0-lb closed-cell).

### Q: Why does cold winter metal or concrete substrate reduce closed-cell drum yield by up to 20%?
A: When spraying 2.0-lb closed-cell foam onto 35°F–45°F steel pole-barn panels or concrete walls, the exothermic reaction cools rapidly against the substrate, increasing foam density from 2.0 lbs/cu.ft. toward 2.4+ lbs/cu.ft. and dropping board-foot yield per drum set from 4,400 down to 3,600–3,850 bd.ft.

### Q: What is a profitable installed price per board foot for open-cell vs. closed-cell spray foam?
A: In 2026, profitable spray foam rigs price 0.5-lb open-cell at $0.48 to $0.62 per board foot ($2.65–$3.40/sq.ft. at 5.5 inches) and 2.0-lb closed-cell at $1.40 to $1.75 per board foot ($2.80–$3.50/sq.ft. at 2.0 inches), targeting 42%–52% gross margin after chemical drums and crew labor.
