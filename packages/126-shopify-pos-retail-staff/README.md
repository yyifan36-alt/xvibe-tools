## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How do I calculate the daily break-even sales target for a retail store or pop-up shop?
A: First sum your daily fixed occupancy cost (Monthly Rent + CAM + Utilities + POS Software ÷ 30) and your daily base payroll (Total Staff Shift Hours × Hourly Wage + Payroll Tax). Then divide that daily fixed dollar total by your net contribution margin per dollar sold (100% − Landed COGS % − POS Card Fee 2.6% − Staff Commission % − Average Return/Discount %). That gives you the exact daily revenue and order count required to break even.

### Q: Why shouldn't retail staff commissions be paid on gross POS register totals?
A: When a sales associate offers a customer a 15% in-store discount to close a sale, your gross margin drops immediately. Paying a flat 5% commission on the gross register receipt—or failing to deduct returned items and sales tax—encourages staff to over-discount merchandise at the expense of store net profit.

### Q: What is a healthy retail staff labor-to-sales ratio for a DTC boutique?
A: For specialty DTC retail stores with 65%–75% product gross margins, total store payroll (hourly base wages + sales commissions + payroll taxes) should stay between 14% and 20% of net store revenue. If a slow weekday shift pushes labor above 30% of sales, reducing overlap hours or shifting to a higher commission accelerator restores profitability.

### Q: How can I evaluate whether a $2,500 weekend pop-up booth is worth the cost?
A: Enter the daily booth fee ($833/day for a 3-day event), setup/teardown staff hours, and your average order value into RetailPOS Studio. You will immediately see how many checkouts per hour your booth team must complete just to pay back the booth fee and product COGS.
