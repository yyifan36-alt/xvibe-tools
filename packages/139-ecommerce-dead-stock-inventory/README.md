### Q: Why is selling dead stock below unit COGS often more profitable than holding it?
A: Unit COGS is a sunk cost. Recovering 75 cents on the dollar today stops monthly storage bleed and lets you reinvest that cash into high-velocity hero SKUs.

### Q: When does Amazon FBA charge the aged inventory surcharge?
A: Amazon charges monthly aged inventory surcharges starting at 181 days in fulfillment centers, escalating sharply at 271 days and reaching $6.90+ per cubic foot at 365+ days.
