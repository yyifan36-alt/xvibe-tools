## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How much dry ice do I need for a 48-hour frozen food shipment?
A: In a 1.5-inch EPS cooler at 80°F with 10 lbs of -10°F meat, dry ice sublimates at ~4.5 lbs per 24 hours. Pack 10.5 to 11.5 lbs for a 48-hour lane so 2 lbs of buffer remains on arrival.

### Q: Why does ice cream require 35% more dry ice than frozen meat?
A: Meat stays safe up to +38°F, whereas ice cream softens and loses texture above +5°F (-15°C), requiring continuous residual dry ice until opened.

### Q: What is the IATA PI 954 2.5 kg (5.5 lb) dry ice limit?
A: Packages with 2.5 kg (5.5 lbs) or less of dry ice use simplified refrigerant markings; air parcels above 5.5 lbs require Class 9 UN1845 labels and $6.75 carrier hazmat fees.

### Q: Why does a 1.5-inch EPS cooler cost less to ship than a 1.0-inch liner?
A: A 1.0-inch liner loses heat 50% faster, requiring +4.5 lbs of extra dry ice ($3.00 ice + $3.80 carrier weight = $6.80 wasted) to save $1.40 on the box.

### Q: Why do frozen DTC brands require $139+ minimum order bundles?
A: With a $26–$32 fixed cooler, dry ice, and Ground label floor, sub-$100 carts lose money. Bundling 6–10 items into a $149+ box keeps shipping under 19% of revenue.

### Q: Does DryIceBox Studio upload our 3PL rate tables?
