## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: What is the difference between Physical Occupancy and Economic Occupancy in self-storage?
A: Physical Occupancy is the percentage of total units (or square feet) currently rented. Economic Occupancy is the actual rent dollars collected divided by Gross Potential Rent at current street rates—accounting for legacy under-market leases, move-in specials, and unpaid delinquent units.

### Q: How do Existing Customer Rate Increases (ECRI) work without causing mass move-outs?
A: Because hiring a moving truck and spending a weekend relocating a full 10x10 unit costs $300+, fewer than 4% of auto-pay tenants move out over a $15 to $22/month rate adjustment applied after Month 6 or Month 9.

### Q: Do self-storage auctions recover the unpaid rent on delinquent units?
A: Rarely. Across the self-storage industry, lien auctions recover only 18% to 28% of accrued delinquent rent and certified notice fees. The real goal of a storage lien process is reclaiming the physical door before Day 60 (often via a 'Pay-Half & Vacate in 48 Hours' settlement).

### Q: Is climate-controlled self-storage more profitable than standard drive-up units?
A: Climate-controlled units command 25% to 45% higher rent per net rentable square foot (especially on 5x5 and 5x10 units), but lose ~25% of gross building footprint to hallways and incur higher construction and HVAC electric costs.

### Q: How much revenue does a self-storage Tenant Protection Plan add?
A: On a $12/month Tenant Protection Plan where the captive program administrator takes $3.60 (30%) and the facility owner retains $8.40 (70%), achieving 80% tenant enrollment across 300 occupied units generates $24,192/year in pure NOI ($372,000 in property value at a 6.5% Cap Rate).
