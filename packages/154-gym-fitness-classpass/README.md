A: Many boutique studios pay instructors a base rate plus a $4.00 to $6.00 per-head bonus above a minimum headcount, and spend $1.25 to $2.00 per attendee on towel laundry, grip wipes, and reformer wear. If dynamic SmartRate drops your off-peak payout to $5.50, your studio loses $1.00 to $2.00 in cash on every aggregator check-in.

### Q: Should studios count ClassPass attendees toward instructor per-head bonuses?
A: Either exclude third-party aggregator check-ins from standard $5/head bonuses (paying a flat $1.50 aggregator head rate instead) or demand a strict $11.00 minimum payout floor from ClassPass so your studio retains at least $4.50 in net contribution margin per spot.

### Q: How do you stop your own unlimited or class-pack members from quitting to book via ClassPass?
A: First, cap peak-time ClassPass inventory at 0 to 1 spot and only release unsold apparatus spots 2 hours before class. Second, restrict repeat ClassPass visitors to off-peak midday times so regulars must hold a direct studio membership to guarantee prime-time reformers.

### Q: What is the ideal ClassPass utilization rule for Reformer Pilates vs. large-room Yoga?

Apparatus-constrained Pilates/Lagree (10–16 beds) has high marginal cost and frequent waitlists, requiring hard 1-spot caps on any time band above 75% direct occupancy.
