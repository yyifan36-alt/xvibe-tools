### Q: Why are self-funded shipping protection programs 75% to 85% net profit margin for ecommerce brands?
A: When 60%–70% of customers add a $2.95 order protection fee at checkout, 1,000 total store orders generate roughly $1,850 in net fee revenue. Meanwhile, typical lost, damaged, or porch-pirated parcel claims occur on only 1.0% to 1.5% of orders (10 to 15 orders), and replacing those orders only costs your landed unit COGS plus a new shipping label ($20–$30 each, or ~$300 total)—leaving over $1,500 in pure net profit per 1,000 orders.

### Q: How much profit do stores lose to third-party shipping insurance apps like Route?
A: Traditional third-party shipping protection apps collect the entire $2.50–$4.50 fee from your customers and keep 80% to 100% of that premium to underwrite claims. For a Shopify brand shipping 3,000 orders per month, switching from a third-party widget to a self-funded protection model typically unlocks $50,000 to $65,000 per year in retained EBITDA.

### Q: Doesn't USPS Ground Advantage, UPS, and FedEx already include $100 of shipping insurance?
A: Yes! USPS Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, UPS, and FedEx automatically include up to $100 of carrier liability for packages lost or damaged in transit. The only gap standard carrier coverage does not pay for is 'Marked Delivered / Porch Piracy'—which is easily funded by a small fraction of your self-funded order protection pool.

### Q: What checkout attach rate should we expect at different shipping protection price tiers?
