### Q: How many full cars can you tint from a single 40"x100' roll of window film?
A: If you use a split-roll setup (a 24" or 36"x100' roll for side doors and a 40"x100' roll only for rear windshields), a 100-ft roll covers ~18–20 rear windows. If you cut an entire 5-window sedan (plus 70% VLT front windshield) from a single 40"x100' roll, each car draws 16 to 21 linear feet—yielding 4.8 to 5.8 complete cars per 100-ft roll after 15% plotter and heat-shrink waste.

### Q: Why is the 70% or 80% VLT full front windshield ceramic add-on the highest-ROI upsell in a tint bay?
A: The customer's vehicle is already cleaned, prepped, and parked in your dust-free bay. Adding a single-piece 70% VLT nano-ceramic windshield consumes ~5.5 linear feet of film (~$28–$34 COGS) and 35 minutes of installer time while adding $175 to $245 to the ticket.
