## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How do you calculate compacted hot-mix asphalt (HMA) tons for a driveway or parking lot?
A: Multiply area (sq.ft.) by compacted thickness in feet (inches ÷ 12) to get cubic feet, multiply by 145 lbs/cu.ft., divide by 2,000 lbs/ton, and add 8% for sub-base grade variance and roller compaction (e.g., 1,600 sq.ft. at 2.5" = 26.1 tons).

### Q: How many square feet does a gallon of mixed asphalt sealer cover on a 2-coat job?
A: While raw manufacturer spec sheets state 80–100 sq.ft./gal per coat, a true 2-coat application (1st coat squeegee/brush on oxidized porous asphalt + 2nd coat spray) averages 52 to 60 sq.ft. of finished pavement per mixed gallon of sand-slurry.

### Q: How many linear feet of cracks does a 30-lb box of hot-pour rubberized crack filler cover?
A: One 30-lb block of ASTM D6690 hot-pour rubber ($48–$58) yields ~180 to 220 linear feet of standard 1/2" x 1/2" cracks. Billed separately at $1.50 to $2.25 per linear foot, crack sealing is the highest-margin service on a sealcoat rig.

### Q: How should we price parking lot line striping, ADA handicap stencils, and fire lanes?
A: On a re-stripe over fresh black sealcoat, charge $6.50–$9.50 per 18-ft stall line, $35–$55 per two-color blue/white ADA handicap symbol, $18–$25 per directional arrow, and $0.85–$1.25/ln.ft. for 4-inch red fire-lane curbing (with a $450–$550 minimum lot charge).
