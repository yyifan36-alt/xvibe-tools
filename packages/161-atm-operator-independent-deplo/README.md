## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: How much vault cash does an independent ATM operator need per machine?
A: On a 10-day self-replenishment cycle with an average withdrawal of $120, a machine doing 360 transactions/month dispenses $43,200/month and requires roughly $14,000 to $16,000 in rotating vault cash float.

### Q: What is a fair ATM commission split with a store owner?
A: For operator-owned and operator-loaded ATMs, standard merchant splits range from $0.50/txn on lower-volume sites (150–250 txns/mo) up to $1.00–$1.25/txn on high-traffic c-stores, dispensaries, and bars (400+ txns/mo).

### Q: When does it make financial sense to use an armored carrier instead of self-loading?
A: Because armored cash-in-transit (CIT) services plus commercial vault cash order fees cost $180 to $320+ per month per stop, armored loading typically requires at least 550 to 700 monthly transactions per site.

### Q: How much interchange revenue do independent ATM deployers earn?
A: In addition to the customer-paid surcharge ($3.00–$4.00), IADs earn net interchange on domestic network withdrawals (typically $0.15 to $0.28 per surcharged transaction and $0.05 to $0.10 on balance inquiries).

### Q: Why should I include vault cash in my ATM ROI calculation?
A: Looking only at the $2,650 Genmega or Hyosung hardware cost inflates your apparent ROI. Since $12,000+ of working capital sits locked inside the cassette and ACH settlement float, true Cash-on-Cash ROI must include both hardware and vault float.

### Q: Does this tool require connecting my ISO portal or bank account?
A: No. All ATM route, vault float, and merchant split calculations run 100% locally in your browser with zero account credentials or server uploads.
