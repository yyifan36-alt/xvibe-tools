### Q: Why should Touch #1 of an abandoned cart flow never include a discount code?
A: Between 35% and 50% of shoppers who recover via a 1-hour abandoned cart reminder simply got distracted or needed to grab their wallet—they already intend to pay full price. Offering a 10% or 15% code in the very first message sacrifices pure margin on those high-intent orders and trains repeat customers to abandon carts on purpose to trigger a coupon.

### Q: How should we structure a profitable 3-step abandoned cart discount ladder?
A: Run a conditional split by cart value and purchase history: Touch #1 (1 hour post-abandon) is a helpful 0% discount reminder with a direct checkout link; Touch #2 (24 hours) introduces a 10% nudge or free shipping for first-time buyers with carts over $60; Touch #3 (48–72 hours) reserves your 15% maximum offer strictly for high-AOV carts ($85+) where post-discount dollar margin remains strong.

### Q: Does sending image/GIF MMS messages convert better than plain SMS in cart recovery flows?
