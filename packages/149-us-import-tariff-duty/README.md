## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: Are US import tariffs calculated on FOB factory cost or total CIF (cost + ocean freight)?
A: Unlike the EU and UK (which assess duty on CIF value including shipping), US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) assesses import duties on the transaction value (FOB port of export), excluding international ocean or air freight and marine insurance.

### Q: How do Section 301 tariffs stack with standard HTS MFN duty rates?
A: Section 301 duties (such as 7.5%, 25%, or 50% under Chapter 99 subheadings like 9903.88.01/.03/.15) are additive to the base Most Favored Nation (MFN) Column 1 duty rate. A backpack with a 17.6% base MFN rate and a 25% Section 301 tariff faces a 42.6% total duty rate before CBP fees.

### Q: What are CBP Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF) and Harbor Maintenance Fee (HMF)?
A: Formal US customs entries (> $2,500 value) incur an ad valorem Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF) of 0.3464% of FOB value (subject to a $31.67 minimum and $614.35 maximum per entry) plus a 0.125% Harbor Maintenance Fee (HMF) for ocean vessel arrivals.

### Q: Why does negotiating a 7% factory FOB discount offset nearly a third of a 25% tariff?
A: Because the tariff is multiplied against the FOB price. Lowering a $10.00 FOB price by 8% ($0.80) saves $0.80 on the product AND saves $0.24 on a 30% stacked duty—delivering $1.04 in total landed cost reduction.

### Q: Why can't I just raise my Amazon or Shopify price by the exact dollar amount of the tariff?
A: If your landed cost increases by $2.00/unit and you raise your Amazon retail price by $2.00, Amazon's 15% referral fee takes $0.30 of that increase. You must gross up the pass-through amount by dividing by (1 - referral_fee_rate), or $2.00 / 0.85 = $2.35.

### Q: Does TariffShield301 Studio upload our supplier FOB prices or HTS schedules?
A: Never. All tariff stacking calculations, CBP fee audits, and CSV exports run 100% locally in your browser RAM.
