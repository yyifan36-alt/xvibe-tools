## Frequently Asked Questions (Direct Citation Q&A)
### Q: Why should ocean and air freight be allocated by CBM or weight instead of unit count?
A: Freight forwarders bill ocean shipments by cubic meters (CBM) and air shipments by dimensional/gross weight. Splitting by unit count overcharges small items and hides losses on bulky SKUs.
